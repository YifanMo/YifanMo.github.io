Quantitative Research & Engineering

  • Cross-Sectional Factor-Based Trading System: independently built a system screening nearly 1,000 perpetual-swap contracts across 3 venues, converting price, volume, open interest, and funding rates into hourly long–short signals. I research economically interpretable factors using historical data beginning in 2021, cross-regime and cross-venue tests, and parameter-sensitivity heatmaps. Reported long-/short-leg return correlation: −0.844 in the Dec. 2025–Jul. 2026 record.

  • Cross-Exchange Funding-Rate Arbitrage System: built funding-rate factors to select assets and exchange pairs, then constructed offsetting positions in the same asset across venues to capture funding-rate differentials. I adjusted positions and risk controls as prices changed to manage liquidation risk on both legs.

My earlier research included CSI 500 index-futures calendar-spread arbitrage (2018–2022) and crypto arbitrage and DeFi yield mechanisms (2022–2024), including liquidity mining and stablecoin staking.

I have also published 57 technical articles on quantitative research and blockchain in the Quantclass community.
