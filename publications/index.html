  1. ISSTA
    Toward Automated Detecting Unanticipated Price Feed in Smart Contract
    Yifan Mo, Jiachi Chen, Yanlin Wang, and Zibin Zheng
    In Proceedings of the 32nd ACM SIGSOFT International Symposium on Software Testing and Analysis, 2023
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    Research summary: Introduces VeriOracle, a formal verification framework that combines price-oracle semantics with smart-contract state to detect unexpected price feeds at runtime. Evaluation covers more than 500,000 transactions from 13 vulnerable DeFi protocols; verification takes about four seconds on average.
