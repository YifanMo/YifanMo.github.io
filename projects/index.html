Cross-Sectional Factor-Based Trading System

Python · Factor research · Backtesting · Portfolio construction · Execution & risk

Nearly 1,000 listed perpetual-swap contracts screened across 3 trading venues · Weekly live-to-backtest reconciliation

I independently built a factor-research and trading system spanning Binance, Hyperliquid, and Aster. Its screening scope covered nearly 1,000 listed perpetual-swap contracts as documented in September 2026. The system turns price, volume, open interest, and funding rates into hourly cross-sectional signals and long–short portfolios.

I studied momentum and relative-performance factors using historical data beginning in 2021, moving from genetic-algorithm factor mining toward low-parameter, economically interpretable factors. Cross-regime and cross-venue tests, together with parameter-sensitivity heatmaps, were used to assess stability. I also compared on-chain screening with market-data signals to refine token tail-risk filters.

Backtests used fixed fee and slippage assumptions. Live execution used order-book-aware order slicing and liquidity controls, with weekly live-to-backtest reconciliation. In the documented Dec. 2025–Jul. 2026 record, long- and short-leg returns had a correlation of −0.844.

Cross-Exchange Funding-Rate Arbitrage System

Funding-rate factors · Asset and venue selection · Cross-exchange hedging

Live-study record: Jun. 2022–Sep. 2026

I built funding-rate factors to select assets and exchange pairs, taking offsetting long and short positions in the same asset across exchanges to capture funding-rate differences.

As prices moved, I adjusted positions and risk controls to manage liquidation risk on both sides. The work centers on funding mechanisms and cross-venue position management.

Honeypot Detection & Transaction Semantics

Research system · IEEE TIFS 2026 · First author

238M+ Ethereum transactions analyzed · 636 attack events identified · 94.6% detection precision

I combined transaction-pattern mining and log-based analysis to study honeypot attacks on high-frequency DApp interactions. The study identified 636 attack events affecting 99 contracts.

The approach recovered 82.4% of failed-transaction semantics for the closed-source trading bots studied and achieved 94.6% precision for honeypot detection. These results describe the research evaluation.

VeriOracle: Runtime Verification for DeFi

Research system · ACM ISSTA 2023 · First author

500K+ transactions evaluated · 13 exploited protocols · 3.85 s mean verification latency

I developed a runtime-verification approach connecting contract code and transaction state with executable DeFi constraints to detect unanticipated price feeds.

In the evaluated protocols, the approach detected every studied incident at or before exploitation. The earliest warning in the evaluation was 33,714 blocks before exploitation; this is the maximum observed lead time. Average verification latency was 3.85 seconds.
